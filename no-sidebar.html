My Career

My career has been defined by adaptability and a willingness to grow beyond my original path. I began in secondary education, teaching high school math for several years before pivoting into higher education administration. For the past eight years, I've been at Texas Tech University, where my work has spanned project management, marketing, social media, and web design, alongside compliance for online higher education in partnership with various organizations. I've built systems within Smartsheet for multiple departments in TTU's Online division and most recently transitioned into the Information Technology division, where I now serve as a GRC analyst on the governance, risk, and compliance team. During my undergraduate studies and the beginning of my career, I've also worked with local small businesses for fifteen years as a marketing director, helping them establish a meaningful online presence. This blend of education, project management, compliance, and marketing experience has made me a versatile problem solver who thrives wherever systems and people intersect.

What I didn't realize at the time was that my varied experience had been quietly preparing me for my next step in pursuing a master's degree in technical communication. Long before I enrolled, I was already doing the work including document design, intermediate web design, dashboard design, technical editing, social media management and design, creating digital elements for emails and social posts, and building well-designed slide decks. The rhetorical side of the field is newer territory for me, and I welcome the challenge of learning it. In the technical classes, though, I thrive by having the chance to grow the skills I've been using intuitively into something more professional and intentional.
